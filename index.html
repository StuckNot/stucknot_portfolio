Coming soon Book · Software development & management

Our first book

Best practice, in depth.

A practical guide to the fundamentals of software development and management, going further than a course can. Written for students and for professionals who are already working in the field.

Most companies do not follow standard practices, and many do not even know what the best practice is. This book sets it out.
Six chapters
  1. Introduction to Software DevelopmentSoftware types, SDLC models (Waterfall, Agile, V, Spiral), engineering principles, requirements and the SRS
  2. Software Design and ArchitectureDesign principles and patterns, SOLID, N-tier and client-server architecture, UML
  3. Software Development PracticesLanguages and frameworks, Git and GitHub, branching, CI/CD, testing and debugging
  4. Software Project ManagementPlanning and scheduling, Agile and Scrum, risk management, team roles and collaboration
  5. Software Quality Assurance and MaintenanceQuality concepts and metrics, code reviews, types of maintenance, configuration management
  6. Emerging Trends in Software DevelopmentCloud computing (SaaS, PaaS, IaaS), AI and ML in software, secure coding and common vulnerabilities

Based on the Fundamentals of Software Development & Management course taught at DSEU, the Delhi Skill and Entrepreneurship University.
